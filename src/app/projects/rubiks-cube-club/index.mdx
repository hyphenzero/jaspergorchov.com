I built the website for my school's Rubik's Cube Club. It's a Next.js site for the LOHS club that meets Mondays and Fridays in Room 223.

The homepage covers the essentials: what the club is, a schedule section with a calendar, a countdown to the next meeting, and browser notifications that fire 30 minutes before it starts. There's a competition section for the first-Friday monthly tournaments, a rundown of what we solve — 2x2, 3x3 from beginner method up to CFOP, Pyraminx, and whatever else people bring — and a team page. Join links go to our ClubHub page.

## Process

The schedule section was the real feature. Instead of a static meeting time, the page has a calendar, a countdown to the next meeting, and a notify-me toggle for browser notifications 30 minutes before it starts.

Everything else is straightforward content: sections for about, competitions, cubes, and team, with photos from actual meetings. The posters at the top of this page are the site's visual identity — the isometric cube on a light grid, with a matching dark version so it works in both modes.
