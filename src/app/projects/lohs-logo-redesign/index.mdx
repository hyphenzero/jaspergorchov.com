The misaligned logo has been used on merch, banners, the website, the giant 3D light-up sign on the third floor, and even the football field — although the football field has a different version that is equally misaligned.

## After

<Image src={posterLight} alt="After" />

The new logo was designed on a 52 × 70 pixel grid so that every element aligns perfectly. All strokes are a consistent 8 pixels wide. The white outline is 3 pixels wide, and the outer blue stroke is 1 pixel. The result is a mark that feels intentional at every scale — from a favicon to a football field.
